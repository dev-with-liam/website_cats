Cat Senses

Cats are predators, so their senses are built for detecting movement, hearing tiny sounds, and exploring their environment.

Eyesight

Cats see especially well in low light. Their eyes also detect motion quickly, which helps them track prey and toys.

Hearing

Cats can hear high-pitched sounds that humans cannot. Their ears can rotate to help locate where a sound is coming from.

Smell

A cat's sense of smell helps it recognize people, places, food, and other animals. Scent is a major part of how cats understand the world.

Why Whiskers Matter

Whiskers are sensitive touch tools. They help cats notice air movement and judge whether their body can fit through a space.

Because whiskers are so sensitive, cutting them can make a cat feel confused or stressed.
