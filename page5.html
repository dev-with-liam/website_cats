Cat Care

Good cat care is about daily routines: food, water, grooming, play, a clean litter box, and regular vet visits.

Healthy Routines

Cats do best when their daily routine is predictable. Regular meals, clean water, playtime, and a clean litter box help them feel safe.

Food and Water

Cats are obligate carnivores, which means they need nutrients that come from animal-based food. Fresh water should always be available.

Grooming

Short-haired cats usually need less brushing, while long-haired cats need more help preventing tangles and mats.

Play

Daily play gives cats exercise and lets them use their hunting instincts in a safe way.

Simple Weekly Checklist

  • Scoop the litter box every day.
  • Wash food and water bowls regularly.
  • Brush the cat's coat, especially for long-haired cats.
  • Check claws and trim only the sharp clear tips when needed.
  • Spend time playing, petting, or calmly sitting with your cat.
